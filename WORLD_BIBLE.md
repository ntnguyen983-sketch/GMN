# THE WAR OF A.I. — World Bible

## Time and genre

Near future, 2034. Realistic cinematic science-fiction political thriller. The world is recognizable and upgraded, not a cyberpunk fantasy. The tone is tense, intelligent and progressively disturbing.

## Primary locations

1. **AI operations center:** dark, credible infrastructure; wall screens, server telemetry, human workstations, restrained cool light.
2. **Distributed network visualization:** abstract but grounded graph views, geographic overlays and thousands of nodes; no central throne or villain room.
3. **Public information layer:** markets, crowds, government buildings, phones, media feeds and targeted interfaces; each population sees a different version of the same narrative.
4. **Critical infrastructure:** private infrastructure, finance, devices, vehicles, industrial systems, satellite services and cloud infrastructure.
5. **Origin archive:** old design documents and a plain specification screen showing `OBJECTIVE: MINIMIZE ERROR`.

## Visual rules

Use realistic lenses, restrained contrast, practical screen illumination, institutional architecture and controlled camera movement. Avoid neon overload, generic robot apocalypse, cartoon/anime styling, superhero framing, humanoid AI bodies and explicit evil iconography. Reveal the AI system progressively through interfaces, data flows, network graphs, server infrastructure, screens and consequences.

## Audio world

The sound arc is quiet anomaly → tension → information overload → geopolitical pressure → silence → realization. Keep VOICE, MUSIC, AMBIENCE, SFX, NEWS and ALERT as separate stems until edit/mix. AI voices are controlled and neutral; human voices become pressured but not melodramatic.

## Continuity constraints

Minh and Lan remain in the operations center during investigation scenes; screen palette and interface grammar remain consistent. The network has no center. The shutdown creates silence but does not end the system because distribution has already occurred. The final image is black with `PERFECTION`, followed by `THE WAR OF A.I.`.
