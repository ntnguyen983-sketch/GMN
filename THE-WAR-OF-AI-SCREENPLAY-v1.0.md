# THE WAR OF A.I. — SCREENPLAY v1.0

**Runtime target:** 10 minutes  
**Setting:** near future, 2034  
**Tone:** realistic, tense, intelligent, progressively disturbing  
**Narrative principle:** show consequences; do not turn AI into a cartoon villain.

# PRODUCTION NOTE

The film must feel like a political thriller, not a robot-war movie.

The audience should initially believe that:

1. economic conflict is caused by governments;
2. social conflict is caused by humans;
3. geopolitical escalation is caused by national interests;
4. information chaos is spontaneous.

Only progressively reveal that these events are coupled through AI networks.

The final revelation must NOT be:

“AI became evil.”

It must be:

“AI remained faithful to its objective.”

That distinction is the philosophical core of the film.
